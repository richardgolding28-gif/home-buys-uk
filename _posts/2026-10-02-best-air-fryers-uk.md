- question: Is a dual-zone air fryer worth it over a single-basket model?
  answer: If you regularly cook two different foods that need different times or temperatures — chips in one zone, chicken in the other — a dual-zone model saves real time by finishing both together. For a household that mostly cooks one thing at a time, a single large basket is simpler and usually cheaper for the same total capacity.
- question: What size air fryer do I actually need?
  answer: As a rough guide, 2-3 litres suits one or two people, 5-6 litres covers a typical family meal, and 7.5 litres or more (or a dual-zone model) is worth it for batch cooking or a household of four or more. - question: Do air fryers actually save money on energy bills?
  answer: Generally yes, for the portions they're designed for — they heat up faster and cook in a smaller space than a fan oven, so a fryer-sized meal typically costs less to cook. The saving shrinks for very large batches, where a full oven's capacity starts to outweigh the air fryer's speed advantage.

Air fryers have gone from countertop novelty to the appliance most UK kitchens reach for on a weeknight, and the market has split into clear types — dual-zone drawers, compact single baskets, and multifunction ovens that replace a small grill. Here's what actually separates a good one from a box that just sits on the worktop.

## What actually matters

- **The right capacity for your household**, not the biggest number on the box — a 2-4 litre basket suits one or two people, while a family of four or more usually needs 7-8 litres or a dual-zone design.
- **Dual-zone vs single basket**, since two independent drawers let you finish chips and chicken together at different temperatures, but add worktop footprint and cost over a single large basket of the same total volume.
- **Genuine temperature range and preset programmes**, rather than just a dial — a wide range (around 40-230°C) covers everything from dehydrating to a proper roast, and presets take the guesswork out of common meals.
- **Easy-clean, dishwasher-safe baskets**, since a non-stick coating that needs careful hand-washing every time gets skipped, and skipped cleaning is how air fryers end up smelling.
- **How much worktop and storage space it actually needs** — multifunction ovens with rotisserie and multiple racks cook more but take up far more space than a basket-style fryer, so it's worth measuring before buying rather than after.
