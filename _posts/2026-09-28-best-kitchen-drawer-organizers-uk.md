A drawer organiser that doesn't actually fit your drawer just becomes another loose thing rattling around. Here's what to check before buying, and which ones consistently fit.

## What actually matters

- **Genuinely adjustable or expandable width** — fixed-size trays are a gamble unless you've measured precisely.
- **Enough depth for what you're storing** — a shallow tray works for cutlery but not for larger utensils; check the tray depth against your actual drawer depth.
- **Material that suits how you'll clean it** — dishwasher-safe plastic for easy maintenance, bamboo if you're prioritising looks and don't mind hand-washing.
- **Non-slip base or grip** — matters more than it sounds; a tray that slides every time you open the drawer is a genuine daily annoyance.
