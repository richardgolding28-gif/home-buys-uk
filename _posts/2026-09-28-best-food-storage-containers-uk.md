Half of food storage containers on the market either leak in a bag or the lids warp after a few dishwasher cycles. Here's what actually holds up, at every budget.

## What actually matters

- **A real gasket seal, not just a snap-fit lid** — the difference between "fine for dry pasta" and "safe for soup in a bag".
- **Stackability with matching lid sizes** — mismatched sets waste cupboard space fast; a proper nesting system matters more than it sounds.
- **Dishwasher-safe in practice, not just on the label** — check recent reviews for warping complaints, especially with plastic lids.
- **Freezer-safe if that's how you'll use them** — glass needs to be specifically freezer-rated to avoid cracking.
