- question: Do I need a full block set, or just a few good knives?

  answer: Forged blades are generally heavier, better balanced, and hold an edge longer, which is why they cost more. Stamped blades are lighter and cheaper and are perfectly fine for everyday chopping — the difference matters most if you're cooking daily rather than occasionally.

A knife block that looks good on the counter but ships with soft, poorly balanced blades ends up with one knife in constant use and the rest untouched. Here's what's actually worth buying, at every budget.

## What actually matters

- **Full tang construction**, where the blade metal runs through the length of the handle — it makes a real difference to balance and how the knife feels mid-chop, especially on the chef's knife you'll use most.
- **Genuine high-carbon or stainless steel**, since softer blades dull fast and need sharpening far more often than the set's marketing implies.
- **A block or storage system that actually protects the edges** — loose knives rattling in a drawer blunt faster than ones stored in slots or on a magnetic strip.
- **The right piece count for how you cook**, not just the biggest number — six knives you'll use beats sixteen where half sit untouched.
