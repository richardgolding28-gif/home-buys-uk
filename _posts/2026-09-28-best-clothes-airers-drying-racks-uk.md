- question: How much washing line length do I actually need?
  answer: As a rough guide, a single wash load needs around 15-18 metres of line space. Check the total line length in the product description rather than judging by the airer's folded size, since panel spacing varies a lot between models.
- question: Heated or unheated — is a heated airer worth it?
  answer: A heated airer noticeably speeds up drying time and helps in poorly ventilated rooms or over winter, but costs more upfront and adds to your electricity bill. An unheated airer in a well-ventilated space is perfectly adequate most of the year.
- question: Indoor or outdoor — do I need a different airer for each?
  answer: A folding indoor airer and a rotary or wall-mounted outdoor line serve different purposes and most households end up with both — the indoor one for wet weather and overnight drying, the outdoor one for faster, cheaper drying whenever the weather allows.

An airer that tips over with a wet towel on one end, or simply doesn't hold a full wash load, defeats the point. Here's what to check, and which ones actually stay stable.

## What actually matters

- **Genuine stability under a full load** — check reviews specifically for tipping complaints with heavier items like towels or jeans.
- **Real total line length**, not just folded footprint — this is what actually determines how much washing fits.
- **Folds flat for storage** if space is tight — worth checking the folded dimensions against where you'll actually store it.
- **Rust-resistant coating** for anything that will live near a bathroom, utility room, or outdoors.
